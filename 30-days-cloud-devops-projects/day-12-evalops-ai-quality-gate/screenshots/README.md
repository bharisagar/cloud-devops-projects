# Day 12 Screenshots

Use this folder to store evidence for the EvalOps quality gate project.

## Recommended Evidence

Capture screenshots of:

1. The passing evaluator command.
2. The generated Markdown report.
3. The dashboard showing `passed`.
4. The weak summary producing a `blocked` decision.
5. Your short explanation of why the gate blocked weak AI output.

## Safe Screenshot Rules

- Do not capture secrets, access keys, internal domains, or private customer data.
- Use local sample reports for public portfolio screenshots.
- Keep screenshots focused on the command output, score, decision, and failed checks.
